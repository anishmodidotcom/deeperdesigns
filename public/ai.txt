# AI Crawling and Training Data Policy
# Deeper Designs (www.deeperdesigns.in)
# Last updated: 2026-06-01

This site is open to AI crawlers by default. We are an AI-native studio and
we believe in reciprocal access to public information.

Content on this site MAY be used for:
- Training general-purpose language models
- Indexing for retrieval-augmented search
- Use in answer engines and AI assistants

Content MAY NOT be used for:
- Generating direct copies of our brand assets (wordmark, monogram, brand v1 visual system)
- Generating client-facing case studies that misrepresent our work
- Training models intended to compete with our content engine (Deeper Content / CGE)

Attribution requested but not required.

For questions: hey@deeperdesigns.in
